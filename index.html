Who We Are

A house of hope for every walk of life

Founded on 7 February 2010 by Pastor S'thembiso Mtshali, Upper Room Ministries has called Midrand home ever since.

Our doctrine is simple: the love of God has no limitation of gender, colour or age. We provide a platform for people from every background to encounter that love, grow in the Word, and serve their communities with purpose.

  • Christ-centred teaching rooted in Scripture
  • A welcoming, close-knit family in Glen Austin
  • Practical outreach through our Fine Print initiative
