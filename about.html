Since 2010

From one room in Midrand to a home for the whole family

Upper Room Ministries was established on 7 February 2010, founded by Pastor S'thembiso Mtshali. The first launch took place in Midrand, and Glen Austin has remained our home ever since.

Upper Room Ministries has been positioned to address the various challenges of society. Our message of hope in God is instrumental in building nations from all walks of life, and our doctrine has no limitation in terms of gender, colour or age.

Community Outreach

The Fine Print Initiative

Fine Print is a forum designed for life-skills development, mentorship and life coaching — a collaboration of efforts towards prosperity, consolidating resources and skills to empower ourselves and the generations to come.

From dignity-pack donation drives at local schools to safe-space conversations around abuse, healing and restoration, Fine Print meets people exactly where they are.
