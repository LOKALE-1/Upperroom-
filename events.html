Fine Print Sessions & Outreach

Beyond our weekly services, our Fine Print initiative regularly hosts life-skills sessions, mentorship circles and community donation drives — including one-on-one counselling for those affected by abuse, and dignity-pack drives at local schools.

Dates for the next session are shared closer to the time — follow us on Facebook, Instagram or TikTok, or get in touch, so you never miss one.
